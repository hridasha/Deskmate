# Dunder Mifflin Office Handbook

## Printing
Each employee gets 200 free color pages per month. Black-and-white printing is unlimited. Large jobs over 500 pages must be approved by Angela Martin.

## Leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid leave per year. Requests should be submitted two weeks in advance. Unused leave expires on March 31.

## Parking
Parking permits cost 45 dollars per month. The north lot is reserved for visitors. Permits are renewed every January at reception.

## Expenses
Meals on business trips are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day. Receipts must be filed within 14 days. Michael Scott approves any single expense above 500 dollars.
